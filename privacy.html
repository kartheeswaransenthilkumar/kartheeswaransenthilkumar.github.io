Privacy Policy

AI News Summarizer

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. About the Application

AI News Summarizer is an educational automation project that collects publicly available AI news and event information, generates summaries using AI, and sends newsletters through Gmail.

2. Information Accessed

The application requests Google account authorization to send newsletter emails. Access is limited to the permissions requested and granted through Google OAuth.

3. How Information Is Used

Authorized Gmail access is used to send AI-generated newsletters to designated recipients. The application is not intended to read personal inbox messages.

4. Third-Party Services

The application uses n8n for automation, Google Gemini for content generation, SerpAPI for search results, and RSS feeds for publicly available news.

5. Data Storage and Security

Workflow data and authorization credentials are handled through the configured automation platform. Execution records may be retained according to the platform's configuration.

6. Revoking Access

Users may revoke Google account access at Google Account Connections .

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact the application developer through the contact information published on the project website.
